Questions

      
Can't find your answer? Ask the team when you send your request.

    

    

      
Is a ski pass included?
No. Ski passes are separate and must be bought before your lesson starts.

      
Which lesson should I choose?
Complete beginners do well in a beginner group, or in a private lesson for faster progress. Families with mixed abilities often choose private lessons so everyone stays together. Experienced skiers can join intermediate or advanced groups, or book private coaching.

      
What if I need to cancel?
48 hours or more: full refund. 24 to 48 hours: 50% refund. Under 24 hours: no refund. If weather cancels your lesson, reschedule or take a full refund.

      
Can I choose my instructor?
You can ask. We'll note it as a preference and do our best, but we can't guarantee a specific instructor because it depends on scheduling.

      
Which languages do you teach in?
English as standard. Spanish, French, Italian and German are available on request.

      
Who do I contact about safety or injuries?
On the mountain, contact ski patrol. Put any medical considerations in your special requirements when you book.
